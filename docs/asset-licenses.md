## Original project content

Sable GT fictional unbranded sedan shell/wheels/sidearm, warehouse kit, container, streetlamp, fence, crane and cargo assembly are modeled reproducibly in art/build_assets.py. Editable Blender sources are included for major assets. The car has actual boolean-cut arches and lofted body/cabin panels; environment assets include modeled industrial hardware, not unchanged Godot blockout meshes. They are early production assets with visible limitations, not final AAA art.

Engine/tire/harbor/footstep/shot/reload WAV assets are original synthesized sounds generated by art/build_audio.py. No external recordings, voice, commercial music, logos or licensed vehicle brands are included. Project-owned content is not assigned a general third-party reuse license by this document.

## Physics and acting additions

Vehicle pane/bumper separation, original damage shaders, crash/glass synthesis and conservative facial morphs are project-authored modifications to the existing original/CC0 assets. Reproduction: art/rebuild_sable.py, art/add_facial_controls.py, art/build_crash_audio.py and art/build_dialogue.py. Six original-text dialogue WAVs use upstream eSpeak NG 1.52.0 built-in formant voices en-us+m3 and en-gb+m1, without MBROLA or recorded voice cloning. The GPL build tool stays ignored and is not distributed. See assets/licenses/draft-dialogue-provenance.txt and espeak-ng-COPYING.txt for exact source, hash and output provenance. These are draft voices, not production performances.
