# The City That Sold Its Soul

Planned arc: Adrian follows Mara's disappearance from harbor payments to a private-security conspiracy. Her ledger links public redevelopment to displaced communities and hidden Blackwater contracts. Each alliance should trade immediate help against later trust.

Act I Homecoming: family and investigation. Act II The Price of Loyalty: competing faction obligations. Act III Ghosts in the Ledger: Mara's evidence and security network. Act IV City on Fire: district consequences. Act V The Last Deal: Exposure, Power or Loyalty, with distinct epilogues. All beyond the initial ledger recovery are design targets, not playable acts.

The first mission introduces Dante's garage and a note pointing toward Elena and Blackwater. No voice performance or authored cutscene currently exists. StoryLine was inspected as a planning reference only; no code integrated.
